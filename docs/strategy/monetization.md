# Compare Empire: monetisation strategy

Researched 2026-10-22. **Not legal, tax or financial advice.** Commission rates and
network thresholds change often. Anything marked **[unverified]** came from third-party
aggregators or conflicting sources. Confirm it in the network dashboard or the program
terms before relying on it.

Hard product rule, repeated throughout: **money never changes the ranking.** Results
are sorted cheapest first. Any paid or affiliate element is either a label on a row
that keeps its place, or a separate, clearly labelled box outside the ranked list.

## 1. Affiliate programs

### How affiliate payments work for an individual with no ABN

Most AU networks (Commission Factory, Impact, Partnerize, Awin, Rakuten) let an
individual over 18 join. The cost of having no ABN is **withholding, not refusal**:

- Commission Factory asks at sign-up whether you are registered with the ATO. If you
  have no ABN and no exemption, it **withholds 47% of payments and sends that to the
  ATO** ([CF tax FAQ](https://help.commissionfactory.com/affiliate-tax-and-billing-faqs),
  [Adding your ABN](https://help.commissionfactory.com/adding-your-abn)). The amount
  withheld is credited against your tax when you lodge your return. It is not lost,
  but it ties up cash.
- If the activity is genuinely a hobby, you can give the payer the ATO's *Statement by
  a supplier not quoting an ABN*, which avoids the withholding
  ([ATO form](https://www.ato.gov.au/forms-and-instructions/statement-by-supplier-not-quoting-an-abn)).
  A site run to make money from day one is probably **not** a hobby (see §5).
- **Practical conclusion:** a sole-trader ABN is free and quick to get. Get one once
  you are seriously applying to programs (see §5).

### Phone plans (much higher payouts per conversion)

The big three (Telstra, Optus, Vodafone) don't run open public affiliate programs for
small publishers. Their payouts go to comparison partners such as Canstar Blue,
WhistleOut and Finder through commercial deals **[unverified]**. MVNOs pay fixed
bounties on Commission Factory:

- Boost Mobile: up to $8.50 per sale, SIMs excluded ([CF](https://www.commissionfactory.com/advertiser-directory/boost-mobile-affiliate-program/73483))
- E.Tel (Optus network): $30 per approved mobile plan ([CF](https://www.commissionfactory.com/advertiser-directory/e-tel-affiliate-program/93449))
- TeleChoice: $10 per sale, and affiliates must not mention Telstra ([CF](https://commissionfactory.com/advertiser-directory/telechoice-affiliate-program/35997))
- Southern Phone: 50% of the first payment ([CF](https://commissionfactory.com/advertiser-directory/southern-phone-affiliate-program/48477))

A single SIM-only conversion ($10–30) is worth as much as a 1% commission on a
$1,000–3,000 phone. A "Pair this phone with a SIM plan" module is the highest-value
affiliate surface. It must sit **outside** the phone price ranking, or be a separately
sorted plan list.

### Insurance and trade-in

- **Phone insurance:** promoting general insurance can involve AFSL or
  authorised-representative rules (Corporations Act general-advice regime). Factual
  referral links are usually handled under the insurer's own licence, but **get advice
  before promoting insurance products**. Defer.
- **Trade-in** (Mobile Monster, Boomerang Buyback, retailer trade-in): look in the CF
  and Impact directories **[unverified]**. A good fit is a "Sell your old phone" link on
  each phone page.

**Who accepts individuals with no ABN:** every network listed above accepts
individuals, and Amazon and eBay accept them directly. Without an ABN or a hobby
statement, AU-based networks withhold 47%. Direct retailer programs (HN, TGG) may
insist on an ABN or business details **[unverified]**.

## 2. Display advertising

- **AdSense:** no traffic minimum, but the site needs original content and the
  policy pages. An individual verifies identity and address (a PIN letter) and fills
  in the AU tax info in AdSense payments. Payment comes from Google Asia Pacific
  (Singapore), so no ABN withholding applies. The income is still taxable.
  An AU individual without an ABN can be asked for GST status; answer not registered.
  **[verify current AdSense AU tax form]**.
- **Ezoic:** sources conflict. One reports a jump to a 250k monthly minimum in
  February 2026, others say there is still no minimum
  ([Newor Media](https://newormedia.com/blog/?p=1430)) **[unverified]**.
- **Mediavine:** the main program needs about 50k sessions a month. The "Journey"
  tier starts at about 10k (some sources say 1k)
  ([Newor Media comparison](https://newormedia.com/blog/?p=1434)) **[conflicting]**.
- **Raptive:** 25k pageviews a month (down from 100k in late 2025). Under 100k, at
  least 50% of traffic must come from tier-one markets, which includes AU
  ([SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/raptive-drops-traffic-requirement-by-75-to-25000-views/558780/)).
- **Expected RPM** (revenue per 1,000 pageviews) for AU shopping/tech traffic:
  AdSense about $2–8, premium networks about $10–25 **[estimate, unverified]**.
- **Caveat:** ads on a comparison page pull clicks away from affiliate links, and the
  affiliate clicks are worth far more. Put ads on content pages (guides, news) and
  keep them off the price table, or use them sparingly.

## 3. Other models

- **Sponsored "featured" slots:** a retailer or brand pays a flat monthly fee for a
  labelled "Sponsored" card **above or beside** the ranked list. The card never
  enters the list and never changes its order. Price it once traffic exists
  (roughly $100–500 a month at 10k+ visitors **[estimate]**).
- **Price alerts and newsletter:** the strongest retention asset. Offer "Email me when
  the iPhone 17 drops below $X". Sponsorship CPMs for AU niche newsletters run about
  $20–60 per 1,000 subscribers **[estimate]**. Send with Resend, Buttondown or
  MailerLite free tiers. The Spam Act 2003 requires consent, sender identification
  and a working unsubscribe ([ACMA](https://www.acma.gov.au/avoid-sending-spam)).
- **Retailer CPC/lead fees:** PriceSpy, Shopbot and Getprice-style engines charge
  retailers a cost per click (cents to $1+), or take a merchant feed through
  Google-style CPC listings **[unverified per-site pricing]**. This only works once
  we can deliver measurable traffic. Show retailers the /go/ click stats from §6.
  To keep the ranking rule, a paying retailer gets a logo or "verified stock" badge,
  never a position.
- **Data/API licensing:** daily AU retail price history for phones is useful to
  analysts, journalists and retailers' pricing teams. Possible later as a paid CSV or
  API. **Check retailers' terms of use on scraping before reselling their data.**
  Treat this as the highest legal-risk model.
- **Premium accounts** (unlimited alerts, history export): low conversion this early.
  Defer until 100k+ visitors.

## 4. Phased plan by traffic

Assumptions **[all estimates]**: 2 pages per visit. 10% of visitors click out to a
retailer. 3% of click-outs buy, on an average order of $1,200, at 1% commission, so
$12 per sale. Plans: 1% of visitors click a plan module, 5% of those convert, at $15
each.

### Stage 0: 0–1k visitors a month (now)

Switch on: the /go/ redirect route and click logging (§6), the disclosure, privacy and
terms pages, and the alert/email capture form. Apply to Commission Factory, Impact and
Amazon AU. Accept 47% withholding, or get an ABN.

- Phones: 1,000 × 10% × 3% × $12 = **about $36/month**
- Plans: 1,000 × 1% × 5% × $15 = **about $7.50/month**
- AdSense is not worth it yet (2,000 pageviews × $4 RPM = $8).

**Total: about $0–50 a month.** The point of this stage is the infrastructure and the
approvals.

### Stage 1: 1k–10k visitors a month

Switch on: the SIM-plan module, refurb/trade-in boxes, the first newsletter, and
AdSense on content pages only. Get an ABN. At 10k visitors:

- Phones: 10,000 × 10% × 3% × $12 = $360
- Plans: 10,000 × 1% × 5% × $15 = $75
- Ads: 20,000 pageviews × 30% content share × $4 RPM = $24

**Total: about $460 a month** (range $150–800).

### Stage 2: 10k–100k visitors a month

Switch on: Raptive or Mediavine once eligible, sponsored featured slots, newsletter
sponsorships, and direct retailer CPC deals. Consider the move to sole trader with GST
if revenue approaches $75k a year. At 100k visitors:

- Phones: $3,600
- Plans: $750
- Ads: 200,000 × 30% × $15 = $900
- Sponsorship: $500
- Newsletter: 5k subscribers × 4 sends × $30 per 1,000 = $600

**Total: about $6,350 a month** (about $76k a year). The GST threshold is close
at this level.

## 5. Australian legal and tax essentials (individual to business)

**Not advice.** Talk to a registered tax agent once income is real.

- **Hobby or business:** the ATO looks at profit intention, repetition, scale and how
  businesslike the activity is ([ATO: business or hobby](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/starting-registering-or-closing-a-business/starting-your-own-business/before-you-get-started/are-you-in-business)).
  A monetised comparison site probably counts as a business. Either way, **all income
  goes on your individual tax return**: ads, affiliate and overseas payments, as
  business income.
- **ABN:** free at [abr.gov.au](https://www.abr.gov.au), as a sole trader under your
  own TFN. An ABN is needed to carry on an enterprise and to avoid the 47% no-ABN
  withholding. Get one before the first real commission.
- **GST:** registration is required once GST turnover reaches **$75,000** a year
  ([ATO GST registration](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/gst-excise-and-indirect-taxes/gst/registering-for-gst)).
  Commissions paid by offshore companies (Google, Amazon) may be GST-free exports, but
  they still count toward turnover **[check with an agent]**.
- **Records:** keep payout statements, network CSVs and expenses (domain, hosting) for
  5 years. Lodge through myTax with business schedules.
- **Pty Ltd later:** costs about $600 in ASIC fees plus an accountant. It makes sense
  at roughly $100k+ profit, for liability protection, or when bringing in a partner.
  To keep the switch easy:
  - Use a dedicated business email (e.g. hello@compareempire.com via free Cloudflare
    Email Routing to Gmail) for every network, Vercel, GitHub and registrar account.
  - Keep the domain in a registrar account under that email. Note that `.com` has no
    ABN requirement, but `.com.au` needs an Australian presence or ABN.
  - Use a separate bank account for site income from day one.
  - Keep a list of accounts so ownership can be transferred to the company later.
- **ACL and ACCC:**
  - Undisclosed commercial relationships can be misleading or deceptive conduct
    (ACL s18). The ACCC wants affiliate links and sponsorship **easy to identify**
    ([ACCC influencer guidance](https://www.accc.gov.au/business/advertising-and-promoting-your-business/advertising-and-selling-guide/influencer-marketing-guidance);
    [2023 sweep coverage](https://www.accountingtimes.com.au/economy/accc-to-lay-down-law-on-influencers-online-reviews)).
  - Comparison sites must disclose that coverage isn't the whole market and must
    disclose commercial arrangements that affect what is shown (ACCC *Comparator
    websites* guidance:
    [accc.gov.au](https://www.accc.gov.au/business/advertising-and-promoting-your-business/comparator-websites)).
  - **"Was/now" pricing:** a "was" price must be a genuine price the item actually
    sold at for a reasonable period recently. Our "price drop" claims must come from
    our own scraped history and show the date range. Never quote a retailer's
    strikethrough RRP as "was" without saying it is the retailer's RRP
    ([ACCC two-price advertising](https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/advertising-and-promotions/price-advertising/two-price-advertising)).
  - Show "Prices checked [date]; may have changed".
- **Privacy Act 1988:** businesses under **$3M turnover are generally exempt**
  ([OAIC](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-legislation/the-privacy-act/rights-and-responsibilities)).
  A privacy policy is still required by AdSense, every affiliate network and Apple/Google
  rules, and an email list makes it essential. Privacy reforms that narrow the
  small-business exemption are pending **[watch]**.
- **Pages the site needs:** /disclosure (how we earn, the ranking-is-never-paid
  promise, the retailers covered), /privacy (cookies, analytics, the email list,
  affiliate cookies, contact), /terms (prices indicative, no liability, scraping and
  data use), and a contact point.

## 6. Technical recommendations (this codebase: Next.js on Vercel free, no DB)

1. **`src/app/go/[retailer]/[sku]/route.ts`:** a GET route handler that:
   - looks up the product URL from the build's static data (a `data/*.json` import),
     never from a query string, so it can't become an open redirect;
   - adds tracking with a per-retailer template in `src/lib/affiliates.ts`, e.g.
     `{ goodguys: (url) => cfDeepLink(url, CF_ID), amazon: (url) => url + '?tag=compareempire-22' }`;
   - with no affiliate deal yet, passes the raw URL with
     `utm_source=compareempire`;
   - returns a `302` with `Cache-Control: no-store` and `X-Robots-Tag: noindex`.
     Disallow `/go/` in `robots.ts`.
   - Links on pages use `rel="sponsored nofollow noopener"`.
   - Add a `subId` (page/position) so network reports show which surface converts.
     Never put personal data in it.
2. **Click tracking on the free tier, no DB:**
   - (a) Log a structured line in the route (`console.log(JSON.stringify({retailer, sku, ref}))`).
     Vercel's free log retention is short, so this is for spot checks only.
   - (b) **Vercel Web Analytics custom events** (limited on Hobby **[verify]**),
     Plausible or Umami Cloud free tiers, or Cloudflare Web Analytics.
   - (c) The network dashboards themselves are the source of truth for revenue.
   - Recommendation: (b) plus (c). Add a DB or KV only when retailers want CPC
     reporting.
3. **Disclosure placement:**
   - a one-line notice above every price table: "We may earn a commission when you
     buy through links. It never affects ranking; cheapest is always first. [How we
     make money]";
   - a footer link to /disclosure;
   - a "Sponsored" label on any paid card.
   - Add a test that asserts the table sort is purely by price, so the ranking rule
     is enforced in code.
4. **Vercel Hobby plan:** Hobby is licensed for **non-commercial use only**
   ([Vercel fair use](https://vercel.com/docs/limits/fair-use-guidelines)). Once ads
   or affiliate income start, upgrade to Pro (US$20/month) or move to Cloudflare
   Pages, which allows commercial use on its free tier **[verify current terms]**.
   **This conflicts with the $0 goal. Decide before switching on revenue.**
