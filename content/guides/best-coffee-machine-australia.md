Australians take coffee seriously, and the machine you need depends less on budget than on how hands-on you want to be. Prices below update every morning from The Good Guys, JB Hi-Fi, Betta, Costco and the brands' own stores.

## Which type of machine?

| Type | Good for | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| Pod / capsule | One button, no mess, consistent cups | Pods cost far more per cup than beans |
| Manual espresso | The best coffee for the money, café-style milk | You grind, tamp and steam yourself |
| Fully automatic | Fresh beans at a button, often milk too | Dearer to buy, harder to fine-tune |
| Drip / filter | Big batches of black coffee | No espresso or frothed milk |

## What actually matters

- **A built-in grinder.** Fresh-ground beans make the biggest difference to taste. A machine without one needs a decent separate grinder, which adds to the real price.
- **Milk.** A steam wand gives the best texture once you learn it; automatic milk systems are easier but need cleaning after each use.
- **The colour code.** The same machine has a different model code per colour, and stores discount colours differently. Check each colour's price on the machine's page.
- **Running costs.** Beans cost a fraction of pods per cup. Add filters, descaler and cleaning tablets either way.
