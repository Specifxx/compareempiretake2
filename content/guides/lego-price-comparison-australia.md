## How to pay less for LEGO

- **Search by set number.** Typing "75447" finds the exact set; typing the name finds look-alikes and older versions.
- **The recommended price is a ceiling.** Toy stores often run 20–40% off popular sets, and the biggest drops come around Black Friday, the toy sales in July and after Christmas.
- **Buy before a set retires.** Sets usually leave shelves after two or three years, and retired sets climb in price. If a set you want has been out a while, the next sale may be its last.
- **Check eBay for sealed sets.** We show new, Buy It Now listings from Australian sellers only, with postage included, so the price you see is the price you pay.
