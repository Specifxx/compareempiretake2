## What to check

- **Body only or kit.** A kit includes a lens, often a basic one. If you'll buy a better lens anyway, the body alone can be the better deal.
- **Australian stock.** Grey-import camera bodies are common online. They're cheaper partly because the manufacturer's Australian warranty may not apply. [More on grey imports](/guides/grey-import-vs-australian-stock).
- **Combos.** Drone "Fly More" and camera "Adventure" combos add batteries, chargers and accessories. Compare combo with combo.
- **Cashback.** Camera makers run seasonal cashback offers through Australian retailers, which can change which store is cheapest after the rebate.
