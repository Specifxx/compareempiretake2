Foldables have gone from novelty to normal. In 2026 Samsung makes three of them, Apple has its first, and Google and Motorola both have strong alternatives. There are two kinds:

- **Flip phones** fold a normal-sized phone in half so it fits any pocket.
- **Book foldables** open a normal phone into a small tablet.

## Flip or fold?

- **Get a flip** if you want a smaller phone in your pocket, or you mostly use your phone for messages, photos and social media.
- **Get a fold** if you read, work or watch video on your phone, and would otherwise carry a tablet.

## Before you buy any foldable

- **Screen protectors.** The inner screen comes with a protector fitted. Don't peel it off.
- **Care plans.** Foldable screens cost far more to repair than a normal phone's. Read [consumer guarantees and warranties](/guides/consumer-guarantees-warranty-electronics-australia) to know what you're covered for already.
- **Last year's models.** The [Galaxy Z Fold7](/phones/samsung-galaxy-z-fold7) and [Z Flip7](/phones/samsung-galaxy-z-flip7) are still around. They're only a good deal when the price gap is real, so check the table above.
