Phones take great photos, so a camera has to do something a phone can't: blur a background properly, freeze fast action, zoom without smearing detail, or shoot clean in low light. A modern mirrorless camera with a kit lens does all four. Here are the cameras the most stores sell today, with the lowest in-stock price from the stores in your country.

## The best mirrorless cameras

Canon's EOS R50 and R10, Nikon's Z50 II and Z fc, Sony's a6400 and ZV-E10 and Fujifilm's X-T30 and X-T50 are the usual first cameras: light, simple, with lenses you can grow into.

## The best action cameras

For bikes, boards, snow and travel, DJI's Osmo Action and Osmo 360 and GoPro's HERO line are waterproof, stabilised and tiny.

## Body only or kit?

A "body" price includes no lens. For a first camera, buy the kit with a standard zoom lens: it costs much less than buying the lens separately. Add a cheap fast prime lens (a 50mm f/1.8 or similar) later for portraits.

## How to choose

- **Pick a system, not just a camera.** Lenses outlast bodies. Canon RF, Nikon Z, Sony E and Fujifilm X all have affordable lenses.
- **APS-C is the beginner sweet spot.** Full-frame cameras are better in low light but the bodies and lenses cost much more.
- **Video?** Look for no 30-minute recording limit, a flip-out screen and a microphone input. Sony's ZV line and Canon's R50 V are built for it.
- **Last generation** often sells for far less with nearly the same image quality.
