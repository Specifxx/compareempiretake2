Black Friday 2026 is **Friday 27 November**, and Cyber Monday is **30 November**. In Australia the sales start well before that: most big stores run "Black Friday" prices for a week or two, and some start in early November alongside Click Frenzy.

The problem with Black Friday isn't finding a sale sticker. It's that many "deals" are the normal price with a bigger "was" next to it. So this page doesn't list advertised discounts. It lists products whose price **today** is genuinely low: lower than last week, lower than the other stores, or below an RRP that another store backs up. It refreshes every morning, all the way through the sales.

## How to shop Black Friday without getting played

1. **Decide before the sale.** Pick the product (exact model) first, then watch its price. Our product pages chart every day's lowest price, so you'll see the drop when it comes.
2. **Ignore the "was" price.** Compare against what it cost last month, and what other stores charge today.
3. **Watch for special model numbers.** Sale events bring TVs and laptops with slightly different codes that can't be compared directly.
4. **Price match.** If the cheapest store isn't the one you want to buy from, [ask your store to match](/guides/how-to-price-match-australia).
5. **Check the returns window.** Several stores extend returns over the Christmas period; confirm before you buy a gift.
