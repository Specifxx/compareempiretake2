A BBQ is the most Australian purchase there is, and the one where the price gap between stores is widest: the same Weber or Beefeater can be $100 or more apart between Barbeques Galore, BCF, Bunnings and the appliance chains on the same day, and the specialist stores run their own sales calendar. Prices below are read from every store we compare each morning, so the figures are today's and the links go to the full comparison for each model.

## Gas, charcoal, pellet or electric?

- **Gas** is the default for a reason: on in a minute, easy to control, easy to clean. Natural gas (NG) models plumb into the house; LPG models take a bottle. Check which you are buying, because the two are listed as separate products.
- **Charcoal** gives the flavour and the ritual, at the cost of half an hour before you cook and more cleaning. Kettles are the cheap way in; kamados (Big Green Egg, Weber Summit Kamado) hold heat for hours and double as smokers.
- **Pellet** grills (Traeger and others) feed wood pellets by auger to hold a set temperature for hours: the set-and-forget smoker, at a price.
- **Electric** suits balconies and apartments where gas and charcoal are banned.
- **Pizza ovens** (Gozney, Ooni) are a category of their own now, and the most-stocked ones are below.

## Which one to buy

- **A family that cooks outside most weeks:** a four-burner gas BBQ with a hood. Buy the NG version if the house has a gas point outside; it pays for itself in bottles.
- **A couple or a balcony:** a two-burner Weber Q or an electric model.
- **Flavour over convenience:** a charcoal kettle first; a kamado if you find yourself smoking brisket on Sundays.
- **Pizza nights:** a gas pizza oven, and spend what you save on a good peel and a stone.

## Before you buy

- **NG or LPG.** The same model is sold in both; check the listing, because the conversion kit is not free.
- **Assembly and delivery.** A large BBQ ships as a flat pack by freight; some stores charge for delivery and assembly, others include it. Our prices are item prices.
- **Covers and accessories** are where the stores make their margin; a cover is worth it, the rest can wait.
- **Sales.** BBQ prices move with the seasons: the deepest discounts are at the end of summer, not the start. [Black Friday](/black-friday) and [Boxing Day](/boxing-day) are the exceptions.
