## What actually matters

- **Install type.** Freestanding sits anywhere; built-under slides under a bench; fully integrated hides behind a cabinet door you supply.
- **Width.** Standard is 60cm; 45cm slimline models suit small kitchens.
- **Noise.** Under about 44 dB is quiet enough for open-plan living.
- **Water and energy stars.** They decide the running cost more than the purchase price.
