## When to buy

- **Black Friday (27 November) and Cyber Monday** are usually the lowest prices before Christmas on headphones, consoles and TVs. See [Black Friday 2026 tech deals](/guides/black-friday-2026-tech-deals-australia).
- **Don't leave it until mid-December.** Popular gifts sell out, and stores stop discounting when stock gets low.
- **Check the price history.** Every product page shows its price over time, so you can see if a "sale" is really a sale. [How to check price history](/guides/how-to-check-price-history-australia) explains how.
- **Gift receipts and returns.** Change-of-mind returns are a store policy, not a legal right. [Consumer guarantees](/guides/consumer-guarantees-warranty-electronics-australia) explains what is.
