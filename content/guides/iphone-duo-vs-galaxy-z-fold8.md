For the first time you can choose between an iPhone and a Galaxy when you buy a book-style foldable. Samsung now makes two Folds: the wider, passport-shaped **Galaxy Z Fold8** and the bigger, camera-focused **Z Fold8 Ultra**. Apple's **iPhone Duo** went on sale on 23 October 2026. Here's how they compare, with today's prices.

## The key differences

| | iPhone Duo | Galaxy Z Fold8 | Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shape | Wide "passport" | Wide "passport" | Tall book |
| Inner / outer screen | 7.6" / 5.4" | 7.6" / 5.5" | 8.0" / 6.5" |
| Rear cameras | 48MP main + 48MP ultra wide | 50MP main + 50MP ultra wide | 200MP main + 50MP ultra wide + 10MP 3x zoom |
| Unlock | Touch ID | Fingerprint | Fingerprint |
| Stylus | Apple Pencil "coming later" | No S Pen | No S Pen |
| Launch RRP (256GB) | $3,499 | $2,699 | $2,999 |

*Specs from Apple's and Samsung's launch announcements.*

## iPhone Duo: for Apple users

[[phone:apple-iphone-duo|The only foldable that runs iOS. If your Mac, iPad and Apple Watch matter more to you than the price, this is the one. It is also the most expensive by a long way.]]

## Galaxy Z Fold8: the best value book foldable

[[phone:samsung-galaxy-z-fold8|Samsung's new wider Fold is the closest thing to the iPhone Duo for $800 less at RRP. Its outer screen is a normal phone shape, which makes it much easier to use folded than older Folds.]]

## Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra: the best camera and the biggest screen

[[phone:samsung-galaxy-z-fold8-ultra|The pick if you want one phone to replace a flagship and a small tablet: the biggest inner screen here, and the only one with a real 3x zoom camera.]]

## Which should you buy?

- **You use an iPhone and a Mac:** iPhone Duo, if you can find it in stock at RRP. Read [our take on whether it's worth it](/guides/iphone-duo-worth-it-australia) first.
- **You want the best value:** Galaxy Z Fold8.
- **You care most about the camera or screen size:** Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra. It costs more than the Fold8 at RRP, but check the table above: store prices move a lot, and the gap is sometimes small.
- **You want a foldable but not a tablet:** look at a flip phone instead. See [the best foldable phones in Australia](/guides/best-foldable-phones-australia).
