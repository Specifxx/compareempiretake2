Noise cancelling used to be a premium extra. Now almost every pair over a modest price has it, and the real differences are comfort, battery life, call quality and how well the cancelling works on a plane or train. Below are the noise-cancelling headphones and earbuds that the most stores we check sell today, with the lowest in-stock price in your country.

## The best noise-cancelling over-ear headphones

Over-ears cancel the most noise, last longest on a charge and are the most comfortable for long flights. Sony's WH-1000X line, Bose's QuietComfort range and Apple's AirPods Max lead most lists; Sennheiser, Sonos and Bowers & Wilkins compete on sound.

## The best noise-cancelling earbuds

Earbuds trade some cancelling for pocketability. If you use an iPhone, AirPods Pro are the easy pick for their seamless switching; on Android, Sony's WF-1000X and Bose's QuietComfort Ultra earbuds are the usual favourites.

## How to choose

- **Fit first.** The best cancelling in the world does nothing if the seal is poor. Earbuds come with several tip sizes; try them all.
- **Battery life** of 30 hours or more on over-ears is now normal. Earbuds manage 5 to 8 hours, plus more from the case.
- **Your phone matters.** Apple's earbuds pair best with iPhones; Samsung's with Galaxy phones. Sony, Bose and Sennheiser work equally well with both.
- **Last generation is a bargain.** When a new model launches (Sony's WH-1000XM6 replaced the XM5), the old one keeps selling for a year at a much lower price, and it still cancels noise very well.
