Apple finally made a foldable. The iPhone Duo (the phone most of us spent two years calling the "iPhone Fold") went on sale in Australia on 23 October 2026, starting at an RRP of $3,499 for 256GB and running to $5,899 for 2TB. That makes it the most expensive iPhone Apple has ever sold here, and around $800 more than Samsung's Galaxy Z Fold8.

So is it worth it? Our short answer: **it's the best foldable for people who already live in Apple's world, but most people should wait.** Here's why, with today's real prices.

## What you get

According to Apple's announcement, the Duo has:

- A **7.6-inch inner screen** and a **5.4-inch outer screen**, a wide "passport" shape rather than the tall, narrow shape of Samsung's earlier Folds.
- The **A20 Pro chip**, the same as the iPhone 18 Pro.
- **Two 48MP rear cameras** (main and ultra wide), but no separate telephoto camera like the Pro models have.
- **Touch ID in the side button** instead of Face ID, and a front camera hidden under the inner screen.
- **eSIM only**, like the iPhone Air. There's no SIM tray.
- A titanium frame and IP68 water resistance.

Apple says Apple Pencil support is coming later. Until it does, we wouldn't buy the Duo expecting an iPad replacement.

## The case for buying it

- **It's an iPhone that opens into a small iPad.** If you read, edit documents or watch video on your phone, the bigger screen genuinely changes how you use it. iPad-style apps and iMessage, AirDrop and your Apple Watch all just work.
- **It's built like a Pro iPhone.** The chip, the titanium frame and the water resistance mean you're not trading durability for the fold the way early foldables did.
- **Resale.** iPhones hold their value better than any Android phone, and early reports of Duo pre-orders reselling above RRP overseas suggest this one will too.

## The case against (for now)

- **The price.** At {{price:apple-iphone-duo}} you could buy an [iPhone 18 Pro Max](/phones/apple-iphone-18-pro-max) ({{price:apple-iphone-18-pro-max}}) and an [iPad Air](/tablets/apple-ipad-air-11-m4) and still have change.
- **The camera.** No telephoto. If zoom matters to you, the Pro models and the [Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra](/phones/samsung-galaxy-z-fold8-ultra) are better cameras.
- **Stock.** Reports at launch said Apple's own delivery dates for new orders had slipped to January, and stock outside the US was expected to be limited. If you see a store with it in stock in the table above, that's worth knowing; if not, don't pay a reseller's mark-up.
- **First generation.** Apple's first versions of new product types (the first Apple Watch, the first iPad Pro with a new design) have often been followed by a much better second version. We'd expect the same here.

## Our verdict

- **Buy it if** you're deep in Apple's world, you'd use the big screen every day, and you can find it in stock at RRP.
- **Wait if** you want the Pencil, a telephoto camera, or a lower price. A second generation and the first proper discounts are both likely to be worth waiting for.
- **Get a Galaxy Z Fold8 if** you want a foldable now and don't mind leaving iOS.
