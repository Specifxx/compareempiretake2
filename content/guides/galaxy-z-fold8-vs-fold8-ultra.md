For 2026 Samsung made two Folds instead of one. The **Galaxy Z Fold8** is the new one: shorter and wider, with a squarer inner screen. The **Z Fold8 Ultra** carries on the classic tall Fold with the biggest screens and the best camera. Here's which one to buy.

## Price check: both, every store

[[compare:samsung-galaxy-z-fold8,samsung-galaxy-z-fold8-ultra]]

Today the Fold8 starts at **{{price:samsung-galaxy-z-fold8}}** and the Fold8 Ultra at **{{price:samsung-galaxy-z-fold8-ultra}}**. Samsung's RRPs for 256GB were $2,699 and $2,999, so the Ultra costs $300 more at full price. Stores and telcos discount the two differently, so the gap in the table can be bigger or smaller than that.

## What the Ultra adds

According to Samsung's launch figures:

- **Bigger screens.** 8.0 inches inside and 6.5 inches outside on the Ultra, against 7.6 and 5.5 inches on the Fold8.
- **A zoom camera.** The Ultra has a 200MP main camera, a 50MP ultra wide and a 10MP 3x telephoto. The Fold8 has a 50MP main and a 50MP ultra wide, with no telephoto.
- **A bigger battery.** 5,000mAh against 4,800mAh.

What neither has: the S Pen. Samsung dropped stylus support from the Fold line, so if you used the S Pen on an older Fold, it won't work here.

## What the Fold8 does better

- **It's lighter and easier to hold folded.** Its wider outer screen feels like a normal phone, so you can use it closed for most things.
- **The inner screen suits video.** The squarer 4:3 screen is closer to an iPad's shape, good for reading and apps that weren't designed for tall screens.
- **It costs less.**

## Our pick

[[phone:samsung-galaxy-z-fold8|For most people. It does everything a Fold should, is nicer to use closed, and saves you money.]]

[[phone:samsung-galaxy-z-fold8-ultra|If you want your Fold to be your only camera, or you want the biggest screen you can fit in a pocket.]]
