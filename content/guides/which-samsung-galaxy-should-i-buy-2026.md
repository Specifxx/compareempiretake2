Samsung sells more phone models in Australia than anyone else, and the names don't make choosing easy. In 2026 the range splits into four families: the **Galaxy S** flagships, the **Z Fold** and **Z Flip** foldables, and the cheaper **Galaxy A**. Here's how we'd choose, with every price checked this morning.

## Our take, family by family

### Galaxy S26: the one most people should buy

[[phone:samsung-galaxy-s26|Flagship speed and cameras in a size you can use one-handed, starting at 256GB.]]

The S26 is the Samsung we recommend most. It's small, quick, and has the same software support as the Ultra. The S26+ is the same phone with a bigger screen; we'd only buy it on sale. The full breakdown is in [Galaxy S26 vs S26+ vs Ultra](/guides/galaxy-s26-vs-s26-plus-vs-ultra).

### Galaxy S26 Ultra: if the camera and S Pen matter

[[phone:samsung-galaxy-s26-ultra|The best camera Samsung makes, a 6.9-inch screen and the S Pen built in.]]

With the S Pen gone from the Fold line, the Ultra is now the only Samsung phone with one built in. If you take notes or sign documents on your phone, that alone decides it.

### Galaxy Z Flip8: a flagship that folds into your pocket

[[phone:samsung-galaxy-z-flip8|The fun one. A full-size screen that folds in half, with a cover screen you can use without opening it.]]

Compare it with the S26 in the table above: with the Flip8 you're paying for the fold, not a better camera, so buy it because you love the shape.

### Galaxy Z Fold8 or Fold8 Ultra: a phone and a tablet

[[phone:samsung-galaxy-z-fold8|The new, wider Fold. Our pick of the two for most people.]]

Samsung's book foldables are the most expensive Galaxy phones. They make sense if you'd otherwise carry a tablet. [Fold8 vs Fold8 Ultra](/guides/galaxy-z-fold8-vs-fold8-ultra) explains the difference, and [iPhone Duo vs Galaxy Z Fold8](/guides/iphone-duo-vs-galaxy-z-fold8) compares them with Apple's new foldable.

### Galaxy S26 FE: the flagship look for less

[[phone:samsung-galaxy-s26-fe|Much of the S26's look for less. Check the gap to the S26 in the table above: it's sometimes small.]]

### Galaxy A57 and A27: the value picks

[[phone:samsung-galaxy-a57|The best mid-range Samsung: a 6.7-inch screen and a 50MP camera for a fraction of the flagship price.]]

[[phone:samsung-galaxy-a27|Samsung's budget pick with long software support.]]

We compare the A57 with Google's mid-ranger in [Pixel 10a vs Galaxy A57](/guides/pixel-10a-vs-galaxy-a57).

## Our verdict in one line each

- **Most people:** Galaxy S26.
- **Best camera:** Galaxy S26 Ultra.
- **Best value:** Galaxy A57.
- **Most fun:** Galaxy Z Flip8.
- **Phone and tablet in one:** Galaxy Z Fold8.
