## What actually matters

- **Real run time.** Claims are on the lowest setting. On carpet boost most sticks last 10–20 minutes; a swappable battery helps in big homes.
- **The bundle.** One model is often sold with different tools, a second battery or a dock. Compare like with like.
- **Hair wrap.** Anti-tangle brush rolls (Shark's and Dyson's newer heads) matter if you have pets or long hair.
- **Bin size and emptying.** Small bins fill fast; a self-empty base fixes that for a price.
- **Battery replacement cost.** Check it before you buy; it decides how long the vacuum stays worth using.
