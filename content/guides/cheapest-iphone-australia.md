## Tips for buying an iPhone for less

1. **Compare the exact storage.** A "$1,499 iPhone" and a "$1,849 iPhone" can be the same model in different storage sizes.
2. **Colour can change the price.** Retailers sometimes discount one colour to clear stock — use the colour filter on each phone page.
3. **Previous generations drop the most.** When a new iPhone launches, stores clear the old one fast. Save the phone and check back.
4. **Watch for "was" prices.** A sale is only a sale if the price was actually higher recently. Our price history shows you what it really cost last week.
