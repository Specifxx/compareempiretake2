For a gaming PC, the processor matters less than the graphics card, but the wrong one can hold a good card back, and the platform (motherboard socket) you choose decides your upgrade path. Here's what to buy.

## The short answer

- **Best gaming CPU: AMD Ryzen 7 9800X3D.** Its extra "3D V-Cache" makes it one of the fastest gaming processors you can buy.
- **Best value: AMD Ryzen 5 7600X or 9600X.** Six cores, more than enough for most games with a mid-range graphics card.
- **Budget upgrade on an older AM4 PC: Ryzen 7 5700X or 5800X3D.** Drop-in upgrades for existing AM4 motherboards.
- **Intel: Core Ultra 5 and 7 (LGA1851).** Strong for productivity and multitasking; check gaming reviews for the exact chip.

## What the letters mean

- **AMD "X3D":** extra cache, best for gaming.
- **AMD "X":** higher clocks than the non-X version, often close in price.
- **Intel "K":** unlocked for overclocking. **"F":** no built-in graphics (you need a graphics card).
