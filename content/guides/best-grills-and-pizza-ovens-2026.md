Whether you call it a barbecue or a grill, the choice comes down to fuel, size and how much you want to fiddle. Weber, Napoleon, Traeger, Kamado Joe, Masterbuilt, Ooni and Gozney make most of what stores stock. Here are the grills and pizza ovens the most stores sell today, with the lowest in-stock price in your country.

## The best gas grills

Gas is the everyday choice: lights in seconds, holds a steady heat and cleans up easily. Three or four burners suit most families.

## The best charcoal grills and kamados

Charcoal gives the smokier flavour and the hottest sear. A kettle is the classic start; a ceramic kamado holds heat for hours of low-and-slow smoking.

## The best outdoor pizza ovens

Gas or wood-fired ovens from Ooni and Gozney reach 400–500°C (750–930°F) and cook a pizza in 60 to 90 seconds.

[[top:cat=bbqs,spec=Type:pizza oven,q=oven,n=6]]

## Gas, charcoal, pellet or electric?

| Fuel | Best for | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| Gas | Quick weeknight cooking | Less smoke flavour |
| Charcoal | Searing and smoky flavour | Slower to start, more cleanup |
| Pellet | Set-and-forget smoking | Needs power; pellets cost more |
| Electric | Balconies where flame isn't allowed | Lower heat |

## How to choose

- **Check what's in the box.** Some prices are for the grill only; covers, gas regulators and stands are extra.
- **Natural gas or bottled gas** (propane or LPG) models are different products. Buy the one that matches your home.
- **Cooking area** matters more than burner count: measure for the number of people you usually feed.
- **Off-season is cheaper.** Grill prices are usually lowest after summer, which in the northern hemisphere is now.
