## The short answer

- **iPad (11-inch):** the best value. Streaming, browsing, notes, school work and kids. Works with the Apple Pencil (USB-C).
- **iPad mini:** the small one. Reading, travel, one-handed use. Fits in a jacket pocket.
- **iPad Air:** the sweet spot for students and creatives who want a faster chip, a better screen and the Apple Pencil Pro, in 11 or 13 inches.
- **iPad Pro:** the best screen Apple makes (OLED), the fastest chip, and the thinnest design. For artists, video editors and people replacing a laptop.

## What to look at

**Apple Pencil compatibility.** Pencils aren't interchangeable across models. Check which Pencil works with the iPad you're buying before you buy either.

**Storage.** 128GB is fine for streaming and school. Choose 256GB or more if you download a lot of video for flights, edit photos, or play big games. You can't add storage later.

**Wi-Fi or cellular?** Cellular iPads have a SIM or eSIM and cost more, plus a data plan. Most people use their phone's hotspot instead. Our prices show each storage size separately, and Wi-Fi and cellular separately.

**Keyboard.** If you'll type a lot, budget for a keyboard case; Apple's Magic Keyboard is expensive, and third-party keyboards are a cheaper alternative.

## Is an older iPad a bargain?

Often, yes. When a new iPad Air or Pro arrives, stores discount the previous model. A previous-generation iPad Air with an M-series chip is still very fast for almost everything.
