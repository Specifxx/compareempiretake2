## Is 65 inches the right size?

For 4K, a comfortable viewing distance is roughly 1 to 1.5 times the screen's diagonal. A 65-inch TV is 165cm across the diagonal, so it suits a couch about 1.7 to 2.5 metres away. Further back than that, look at a [75 or 85-inch](/tvs) model.

The TV itself is about 145cm wide. Check your cabinet or wall space, and whether the feet sit at the edges (which needs a wide cabinet) or in the centre.
