## What actually matters

- **Basket shape over litres.** A wide, flat basket crisps more food in one layer than a tall one with the same capacity.
- **Dishwasher-safe parts** save the most time over a year.
- **Noise and size.** Measure your bench and the space under your cabinets; big dual-zone models are deep.
- **Extra modes** (dehydrate, roast, bake) are nice, but most people use air fry and reheat.
