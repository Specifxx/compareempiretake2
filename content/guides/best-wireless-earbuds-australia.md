## Which earbuds for which phone?

- **iPhone:** AirPods integrate best (instant pairing, switching between Apple devices, spatial audio with head tracking).
- **Samsung:** Galaxy Buds unlock Samsung-specific features and codecs.
- **Pixel and other Android phones:** Sony, Bose and Google's own Pixel Buds work well; choose on fit and sound.

## What to check

- **Fit.** The best earbuds are the ones that fit your ears. Try them in store if you can, and check what ear-tip sizes are included.
- **Noise cancelling.** Matters most for commuting and flights.
- **Battery:** look at both the earbuds' own battery and the total with the case.
- **Water resistance** (IPX4 or higher) if you'll sweat or run in them.
- **Previous generations.** Last year's model is often heavily discounted and nearly as good.
