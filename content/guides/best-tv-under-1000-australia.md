## What you give up under $1,000

- **Contrast:** most cheaper TVs use VA or IPS LCD panels without many dimming zones. Fine in a lit room, weaker for movies at night.
- **Gaming features:** many budget TVs are 60Hz. If you have a PS5 or Xbox Series X and want 120Hz, check the spec sheet for HDMI 2.1.
- **Sound:** thin TVs have thin sound. A budget [soundbar](/guides/best-soundbar-australia) makes a bigger difference than most picture upgrades.
- **Processing:** cheaper TVs upscale low-quality broadcasts less cleanly. Free-to-air TV in Australia is still mostly broadcast below 4K.

## Brands to know at this price

TCL and Hisense dominate the under-$1,000 market in Australia, with Samsung and LG's entry models alongside them and store brands like FFalcon and Blaupunkt at the very bottom. The big-brand models usually get software updates for longer.
