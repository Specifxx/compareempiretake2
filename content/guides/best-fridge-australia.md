## What actually matters

- **Measure first,** then add the ventilation gap the manual asks for at the sides, top and back.
- **Energy rating.** Compare the kWh per year on the label; over ten years the running cost can outweigh a price difference.
- **Plumbed or not.** Plumbed ice and water needs a tap behind the fridge; non-plumbed models have a tank.
- **Delivery and removal.** Check delivery, installation and old-fridge removal for your postcode before comparing two prices.
