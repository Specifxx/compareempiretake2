## The short answer

- **Best value for most people: iPhone 17.** It starts at 256GB and has a 120Hz ProMotion screen, the two upgrades that matter most day to day.
- **Cheapest way to a new iPhone: iPhone 17e.** The essentials of a current iPhone at a lower price, with fewer camera and display extras.
- **Thinnest and lightest: iPhone Air.** Beautiful to hold, but one rear camera and a smaller battery than the Pro models.
- **Best cameras: the Pro models.** A telephoto zoom, the best video features, and the biggest batteries in the Pro Max.

## What actually differs

**Cameras.** The Pro models add a telephoto lens for zoomed shots without losing detail, plus pro video features. The iPhone 17 has a main and an ultra-wide camera; the Air has a single main camera. For everyday photos, all of them are excellent.

**Screen.** The 17, Air and Pro models have high-refresh ProMotion displays that make scrolling smoother.

**Battery.** The Pro Max sizes last longest. The Air trades battery for thinness; heavy users may want Apple's battery accessory or a Pro.

**Size.** The Pro Max models are big. If you want a smaller phone, the iPhone 17 and 17 Pro are the comfortable sizes.

**Storage.** Current models start at 256GB, which is enough for most people. Choose 512GB if you shoot a lot of 4K video. See [how much phone storage you need](/guides/how-much-phone-storage-do-i-need).

## Last year's Pro is the quiet bargain

When a new Pro launches, retailers discount the previous Pro while they clear stock. The 17 Pro is still one of the best phones you can buy, and it's usually well below the 18 Pro's price. Check both in the table above, and the 17 Pro's price history, before you pay for the newest model.
