## The best party speakers

Party speakers are big, loud and usually have lights, a handle and wheels. They need a power point for full volume and most have a microphone or guitar input for karaoke.

## Ratings, decoded

| Rating | What it survives |
|---|---|
| IPX4 | Splashes and rain |
| IP67 | Dust, and being dropped in water up to a metre for 30 minutes |
| IP68 | Longer or deeper immersion, as the maker states |

Battery life claims are measured at moderate volume. Expect about half at a party.

## How to choose

- **Size decides bass.** Small speakers sound thin no matter the brand; step up one size if you listen outdoors.
- **Pair two** for stereo or a bigger sound: JBL's Auracast, Bose's SimpleSync and Ultimate Ears' PartyUp link speakers of the same family.
- **Wi-Fi or Bluetooth?** Sonos's portables (Roam and Move) also join a home Wi-Fi system, which is worth it if you have other Sonos speakers.
- **Last year's model** usually sounds the same and costs less. Check the price history on each speaker's page.
