## The best Dolby Atmos soundbars

Dolby Atmos adds height to the sound: rain overhead, a plane flying past. You get the real effect from bars with up-firing speakers (the third number in "5.1.2" is the count of them), and it works best with a flat ceiling of normal height. Netflix, Disney+, Apple TV+ and most 4K Blu-rays carry Atmos tracks.

## How to choose

- **Use HDMI eARC.** Plug the soundbar into your TV's eARC port and the TV remote controls the volume, and Atmos passes through. Optical works, but without Atmos.
- **Match the width** roughly to your TV. A bar much wider than the TV looks odd; much narrower and the stereo image shrinks.
- **Dialogue is what most people want fixed.** A bar with a centre channel (3.1 or better) or a voice-enhancement mode solves "I can't hear what they're saying."
- **Stick with your TV's brand** if you want extras: Samsung's Q-Symphony and LG's WOW Orchestra combine the TV's and bar's speakers, and Sony's Bravia bars do the same with Sony TVs.
