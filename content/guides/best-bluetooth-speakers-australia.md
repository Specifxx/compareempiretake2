## By size

**Small (fits in a bag):** speakers like the JBL Go and Flip, the Bose SoundLink Micro and the UE Wonderboom are for personal listening, the shower and travel. Expect decent volume but limited bass.

**Medium:** the Sony ULT Field 3 and 5, UE Boom and JBL Charge fill a backyard and usually last a full day on a charge.

**Large and party speakers:** the JBL Xtreme and Boombox, and party speakers with lights and handles, for gatherings and outdoor events.

[[top:cat=speakers,spec=Type:Party,stores=2,n=6]]

## What to check

- **Water and dust resistance.** An **IP67** rating means dust-tight and waterproof to about a metre for 30 minutes, which covers the beach and the pool. IPX4 only means splash-proof.
- **Battery life** at real volume, not the maximum claim.
- **Pairing two speakers.** Many brands let you link two of the same brand for stereo or more volume, but rarely across brands.
- **Charging port.** USB-C is standard now; some speakers can also charge your phone.
