## How to choose

- **Your graphics card sets the ceiling.** A 360Hz monitor is wasted if your PC runs the game at 90 frames per second.
- **Consoles top out at 120Hz.** For a PlayStation 5 or Xbox Series X, a 4K 120Hz monitor with HDMI 2.1 is the target.
- **OLED care:** modern OLEDs have burn-in protection, but avoid leaving a static desktop on for hours at full brightness.
- **G-Sync or FreeSync** removes tearing. Most monitors support both through "G-Sync Compatible".
