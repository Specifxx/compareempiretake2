A student laptop has a hard life: carried everywhere, used all day, expected to last a whole degree. That makes battery life, weight and durability more important than raw power for most students. Here's what to buy, with today's prices from Australian stores.

## What students actually need

- **16GB of memory.** The single most important spec for a laptop you'll keep for three or four years.
- **All-day battery.** Look for chips known for efficiency: Apple's M-series, Snapdragon X, and Intel Core Ultra or AMD Ryzen AI "U" or low-power chips.
- **Under about 1.5kg** if you'll carry it every day.
- **512GB of storage,** unless you live in the cloud.
- **A good keyboard and screen.** You'll type thousands of words on it. Try one in store if you can.

Check your course first: some engineering, architecture and design courses need Windows software or a dedicated graphics card, and some schools specify a device.
