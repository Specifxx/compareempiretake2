Headphones are one of the most discounted things in tech, and one of the most confusing to compare: the same pair comes in four colours, older versions stay on sale beside the new ones, and "noise cancelling" means very different things at $100 and $600.

We check {{count:headphones}} headphones and earbuds at {{storecount:headphones}} Australian stores every morning, matched by exact model.

## Tips

- **Last year's flagship is often the best buy.** When Sony, Bose or Apple release a new model, the previous one usually drops a long way and stays on sale for months.
- **Colour changes the price.** Stores clear particular colours. If you don't mind which, check each colour's price.
- **Headphone sales follow the calendar.** Black Friday, Prime Day and EOFY see the deepest cuts. See [the best time to buy](/guides/best-time-to-buy-electronics-australia).
