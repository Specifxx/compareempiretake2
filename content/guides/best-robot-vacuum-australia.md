Robot vacuums have changed a lot. The best ones now map your home, avoid cords and pet mess, mop as well as vacuum, and return to a dock that empties their bin and washes their mop, so you touch them once every few weeks. That dock is also where most of the price goes.

## What actually matters

- **Navigation:** LiDAR (a spinning laser on top) maps rooms accurately and works in the dark. Camera-based obstacle avoidance helps with socks, cords and pet accidents.
- **The dock:** auto-empty saves the most effort. Mop washing and drying matter if you'll mop often.
- **Carpet handling:** if you have rugs, look for mops that lift or detach so carpets stay dry.
- **Height:** measure under your couch and beds. Some robots are too tall to fit.
- **Running costs:** bags, filters, brushes and mop pads. Check that spares are easy to buy in Australia.
- **App and privacy:** robots with cameras map your home. Choose a brand with a clear privacy policy and keep the app updated.

## Which brand?

**Roborock, Dreame and Ecovacs** lead on features at most price points. **iRobot (Roomba)** remains widely sold and well supported. **Narwal** specialises in mopping. Brand matters less than the specific model's dock and navigation: compare the features above.
