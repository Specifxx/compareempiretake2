## What to look for under $500

- **5G is now standard.** Only buy a 4G phone if it's under $200 and for a kid or as a backup.
- **128GB minimum.** 64GB fills up with apps and photos within a year.
- **Software updates matter more than specs.** Samsung and Google promise the longest support at this price; that's the difference between a phone that lasts two years and one that lasts five.
- **Check the storage before the price.** Stores often advertise the smallest storage size. We split every phone by storage so you compare like with like.
